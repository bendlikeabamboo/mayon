Decision History

Every feature this project has shipped through its specification process, condensed into one story: what each set out to achieve, why it mattered, where later decisions undid or refined an earlier one, and what we learned along the way. It favors judgment over procedure — for step-by-step acceptance gates from before this process existed, read the development notes; for the design of the system as it stands today, read architecture and seams.

Each feature now has its own page — this page is the map, one table per arc, plus the arc narrative: the intros, transitions, and retrospectives that turn the entries into one story. The twenty-four features fall into five arcs: the agent surface taking shape, the AI plumbing industrializing, platform and delivery polish, trust, gates, and the industrialized practice, and surface, reach, and the docs themselves. Within each arc the entries flow chronologically; reversals are stated openly wherever they happened — including several decisions that were built and then replaced inside their own feature before landing. Contracts the narrative points at are preserved verbatim under appendices/.

Arc A — The agent surface takes shape

Mayon gained its first external tool, then rebuilt the surface where tool activity appears. A web-search integration produced a flood of raw tool rows; typed timeline entries made that flood legible; live use exposed what fixtures could not catch; and a contract pass converged every assistant-initiated interaction into one governed place.

Arc A in retrospect: the surface learned to tell the truth in layers — first structural truth (typed entries), then behavioral truth (ordering, pairing), then contractual truth (where interaction may live and what diagnostics may say). Every later arc inherited those contracts instead of rediscovering them.

Arc B in retrospect: every entry traded ad-hoc branching for data, derivation, or adoption — shapes over identities, catalogs over code, one resolver over scattered maps, user-managed strings over constants. Notably, the arc shipped almost entirely additive to Arc A’s contracts, validating them as a stable foundation. The one deep rewrite — request-settings resolution — earned its deletion by replacing code the dialect research proved defective.

Arc C — Platform & delivery polish

This arc widened who can install Mayon and made the product legible at a glance — and both features carry decision reversals handled the way we now consider standard: built, evidenced, reviewed, and calmly revised.

Feature Status
011 — Podman compatibility standing — one same-session scope reversal

Arc C in retrospect: neither feature moved a schema; both changed how much of the system users can see and steer. The repeated pattern — build the strong form, capture evidence, let an explicit ruling trim it — is the project’s decisive-method matured: reversals stopped being failures once they stopped being expensive.

Arc D — Trust, gates & the industrialized practice

By late August the practice itself became the feature. The specification archive — twelve feature directories and 133,926 words — was consolidated into the decision history you are reading, kept honest by a fixed template, fixed budgets, and an audit-to-green gate before any deletion. Mayon went onto the public internet behind one authentication gate enforced once at the server seam, its uniformity proven by a CI sweep test rather than per-route discipline. The chat experience gained an end-to-end suite driven by a protocol-exact mock LLM — no live provider, no API key, zero product diff. The freshly public repository gained always-on, report-only security posture detection. And the manual release-candidate pass became automated verification: the mock learned to discriminate request kinds and grade deterministically while a real browser drove the real app.

Arc D in retrospect: trust was made structural, never per-route discipline — one hook covers every endpoint and a sweep test keeps it one; deletion waited on audit gates going green; and “enabled” in a settings API counted only after live adversarial tests proved it enforcing, with the degraded leg accepted and recorded rather than papered over. The arc legislated its own restraint too: report-only detection, no scheduled update floods, and gates that never block on unproven signal — a solo maintainer’s noise budget treated as a design constraint. Splitting this history into per-feature pages is the same discipline applied to itself.

Arc E — Surface, reach & the docs themselves

The final arc widened what the surface can do and how far the product reaches — and made the docs themselves into a shipped feature. Settings gained navigation around a deliberately frozen page; a six-card idea deck traded the fully designed floating reply outline for its leaner sibling, the section peek strip, which shipped the panel’s contract primitives instead of forking them; GitHub Copilot brought the workplace main AI onboard as the first provider kind defined by auth rather than wire format; image chat parts gave vision a parts-based contract under which voice, video, and files become follow-on slices rather than a rewrite; branched chats learned to push their outcomes back into the parent as anchored artifacts; and the documentation was restructured into the Diátaxis website this page is published on.

Arc E in retrospect: reach arrived without rewrites — a new modality became a nullable parts column beside canonical text, a new provider became a kind behind the existing keystore and resolver, and branch back-propagation became one more entry kind in the model 002 — Chat timeline kind model built, riding abstractions earlier arcs had already paid for. It is also the record’s most honest stretch: 015 was fully designed and never built, its contract realized by a sibling that consumed it, the floating panel kept on the books as the recorded upgrade path — declined winners recorded as upgrade paths, not dead ends.

Where the story stands

Twenty-four features in three weeks of calendar time, with one recurring shape: invent narrowly, prove broadly, revise without ceremony. The durable mechanics — typed entries, derived states, written contracts, catalog-driven extensibility, offset-sanctified selection, evidenced styling rulings — now function as the platform every future feature inherits. Arcs D and E hardened and widened that platform: releases ship through automated gates driven by a protocol-exact mock, the product sits behind one centrally enforced authentication gate with always-on security posture detection, reach grew through Copilot, image parts, and branch back-propagation, and the documentation became the Diátaxis website you are reading. Not everything is built, and the record says so: the floating reply outline remains what a where-am-I marker on the strip would grow into; voice, video, and file support stay recorded as future work, not abandoned; and push protection’s enforcement gap is a recorded follow-up. The know-how distilled here is deliberately opinionated; when a future change contradicts an entry here, update that entry in the same change — this history is a living decision record, not a museum.
