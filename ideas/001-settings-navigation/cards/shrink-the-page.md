# Card 003 — Shrink the page

## Story

You ship a pass that cuts the page down instead of navigating it: rarely-touched sections collapse under "Advanced", and one-off actions like backup/restore move next to the data they act on. What remains fits in roughly a screen, so there is almost nothing left to get lost in — finding backup stops being a scrolling problem because backup isn't buried at the bottom anymore. Navigation is treated as a symptom; length is the disease.

## Playthrough (2026-08-28)

- **What & why (confirmed at deal time)**: What — reaching any specific settings area, like Backup, is quick and self-evident, without scrolling past everything else. Why — the settings page is one long scroll today; length plus the cognitive load of scanning every section makes finding a known section slow and frustrating.
- **How it goes**: The first days are triage, not nav-building — sections sorted by touch frequency; the lab/quiz/expound prompt configs fold under an "Advanced" disclosure, the learner profile stays open, and Data moves up (renamed so "backup" is findable near the top). The page lands at roughly two screens and finding becomes glancing. From then on the pattern leans on judgment: every new "where does this go" ruling is a decision about what deserves visibility, and the page stays short only while those decisions stay honest.
- **Snags**: Wrong triage taxes you daily — collapse a section you actually touch weekly and you pay on every visit. Collapsed sections hide state: errors inside drawers don't announce themselves. The fix is consumable, not structural — "Advanced" slowly becomes its own long page as settings accumulate. Discoverability of tucked-away features drops; you forget the quiz prompt config exists.
- **Trade-offs**: Glanceability of everything at once — that's the point, but it's a real give-up. No URL/deep-link story. Moving backup "next to the data it acts on" has no natural home, since backup acts on everything — that half survives only as a design decision, not a free win.
- **Delivers the what?**: Partially — fully for known, frequently-touched areas if the grouping is right (backup one glance away within days); not self-evident for collapsed areas, where reaching a section requires remembering which drawer holds it.
