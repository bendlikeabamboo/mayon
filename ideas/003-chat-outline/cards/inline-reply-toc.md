# Card 003 — Inline "In this reply" TOC

## Story

Any reply past a length threshold renders a compact "In this reply" list of links right at its top. You scan the list, click "Costs", and land at that section, then keep scrolling the way you do today. Nothing floats over the chat; the overview lives where your eyes already start.

## Playthrough (2026-09-02)

- **What/why checked against**: jump to the part of a long reply without scrolling all of it; long header-heavy replies are hard to search in the UI today.
- **How it goes**: You ship it and the opening moment improves immediately — a long reply loads, you scan its shape in one glance, click "Costs", and start reading in the right place. The trouble starts mid-read: once you're four sections down, the TOC is three screens up, and the second jump means scrolling back or a "back to top" hop. Someone inevitably asks for the list to stay visible while scrolling — which is Card 001 re-entering through the back door. Meanwhile the threshold question nags: too eager and every medium reply wears a TOC nobody asked for, too shy and the monsters that need it don't get one.
- **Snags**: repeated navigation unserved — every jump after the first — the core miss; requests for stickiness — week one of dogfooding — pressure that mutates the card into Card 001; threshold tuning — ongoing — constant small noise either way; adds height to already-long replies — day one — mildly ironic, collapsible-by-default softens it but hides the feature.
- **Trade-offs**: gives up all mid-read and ambient navigation; buys total quiet — no floating chrome, no scroll-spy, no exclusions.
- **Delivers the what?**: partially — orientation and a first jump at the start of a reply, yes; "speed up searching" in the middle of one, no.
- **Difficulty vs payoff**: difficulty S · payoff M · time-to-first-value days
- **Your take**: "Not much payoff and doesn't solve the problem" — rejected; the mid-read case is the real one. Moved on to Card 004.
